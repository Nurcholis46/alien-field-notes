## Why #8 won

- **On-subject (5):** unmistakably an alien tourist's review of Earth — "came to study humans".
- **Surprise/twist (5):** three escalating reversals in one sentence: the researcher gets adopted (by a dog, not the other way round), ends up owing a mortgage, and is emotionally invested in a turtle named Gerald. The proper name makes the absurdity concrete.
- **Authentic voice (5):** reads exactly like a travel review — five stars, a verdict, a closing remark ("No refunds").
- **Memorable (5):** "a turtle named Gerald" is the detail a judge remembers after reading 3,000 lines.
- **Gate (5):** 157 characters, single line, UTF-8, non-whitespace.
- **Judge-fit:** one joke landed cleanly (the alien got absorbed by Earth life), punch on the last two words, no stacked jokes, no explanation of the joke, and it avoids the brief's named obvious topics (traffic, gravity, money as the joke itself, weather).
